## BULLET STRENGTH CHECKLIST

Every bullet in this resume should have:
- ✅ Strong action verb (not "responsible for", "helped", "worked on")
- ✅ At least one number/metric (percentage, count, time, money, scale)
- ✅ Specific outcome or result (what changed because of this work?)
- ✅ Context of scale (users, dataset size, team, budget, etc.)
- ✅ 1-2 lines maximum when written
- ✅ Reads as an achievement, not a duty
- ✅ Relevant to target role (Full-Stack Data Engineer)
